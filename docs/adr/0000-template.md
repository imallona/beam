# 0000 - Template for architecture decision records

- Status: Template
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: -
- Supersedes: -
- Superseded by: -

## Context

What is the issue? What forces are at play (technical, organisational, social)? Short.

## Decision

What did we decide? State the decision in one to three sentences.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder because of this decision? Cover positives and negatives. Be fair about costs, particularly in terms of time or science.

## Alternatives considered

What other options were on the table? One sentence each on why each was rejected.

## References

Links to related ADRs, papers, code, or external docs that fed the decision.
